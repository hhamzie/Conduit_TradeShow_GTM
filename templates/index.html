Trade Show Ops

Pick what you want back.

If you want one CSV now, use the single-show scraper. If you want many CSVs fast, use the bulk path. If you want the app to manage Clay and Smartlead for you, use the automation lane.

3 ways in

All three flows keep the website/domain-only guardrail. The first two are immediate scrape tools. The third is the longer-running outbound workflow.

How To Choose

Use the lane that matches the output you want, not just the input you have.

Fastest

One show, one CSV

Use this when you have a single directory URL and just want the cleaned company export back right away.

Batch

Many shows, many CSVs

Use this when you have a spreadsheet of trade shows and want a ZIP file with one CSV per show plus a manifest.

Outbound

Clay plus Smartlead automation

Use this when you want the app to own the pipeline after scraping: Clay enrichment, Smartlead campaign prep, and launch control.
